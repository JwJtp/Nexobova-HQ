About Nexobova HQ

Nexobova HQ — AI-era business software from Bangalore

Nexobova HQ (also written Nexobova) is a business software and digital solutions company in Bangalore, Karnataka, India. We connect CRM, GST billing, ERP, POS, HR, WhatsApp automation, AI chatbots, 3D product viewers, websites and custom apps into one platform — set up for you, with a futuristic AI-era vision and a premium, professional finish.

What is Nexobova HQ?

Nexobova HQ (also written Nexobova) is a business software and digital solutions company in Bangalore, Karnataka, India. We build and set up connected apps — CRM, GST billing, ERP, POS, HR, WhatsApp automation, AI tools, 3D showrooms, websites and custom apps — with a premium, professional finish and an AI-era vision.

Where is Nexobova HQ located?

Nexobova HQ, JP Nagar, Bangalore, Karnataka 560078, India. We work with businesses across India and worldwide.

What does Nexobova HQ offer?

A suite of connected business apps (Nexobova One), custom websites, portals and mobile apps built to your process (Nexobova Studio), and AI agents for WhatsApp and the web (Nexobova AI). Every project is set up for you, with scope and price agreed in writing.

Is Nexobova billing software GST-ready?

Yes. Billing & GST supports GST invoices, UPI / GPay payment links, dues tracking and recurring invoices, and connects to Accounts and Inventory.

Does Nexobova HQ build custom websites and apps?

Yes. Nexobova Studio designs and develops business websites, landing pages, custom web apps and Android and iOS apps, with fixed scope and price in writing and enquiries flowing straight into your CRM.
